  • It is now confirmed that Kusunoki No. 2 Housing is national property of the Ministry of Finance, administered by the Shikoku Financial Bureau. But the core question remains

  • The residential address in the CSV is a representative notation per account; per-building numbers are not listed. Moreover, the representative notation for No. 2 Housing is “1-1 et al.”, which appears to differ in block number from the “2-1-2” premise of the X post. What the residential number of Building No. 2 actually is — and whether building numbers are used under the mid/high-rise exception — can only be confirmed in the city’s residential address registry.
  • That sharpens the question for the call to the city: “The public ledger lists No. 2 Housing as 1-1 et al. — what is the residential number of Building No. 2?” can now be asked head-on.
